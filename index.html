Hi, I’m Ido. I’m a final-year undergraduate in Philosophy, Politics and Economics (PPE), with a minor in Law, at Tel Aviv University.

I’m interested in decision theory, game theory and information economics, social choice, and forecasting — both its theory and its practice (see the election model below).

Alongside my studies, I work as a Decision Scientist on PayPal’s Partner Risk team, building statistical fraud-detection and anomaly-detection models. Before that, I was a research assistant to Dr. Moran Lazar at TAU’s Coller School of Management, where I worked on a study of how human–AI collaboration shapes business ideation (with H&M), which became a Harvard Business School working paper.

Before university, I served as a platoon sergeant in the IDF’s Maglan unit. I later held a student position at the Prime Minister’s Office.
