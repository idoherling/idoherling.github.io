About

I am a final-year undergraduate in Philosophy, Politics and Economics (PPE), with a minor in Law, at Tel Aviv University. I am interested in decision theory, game theory and information economics, social choice, and the theory and practice of forecasting. I plan to pursue graduate study in economics.

Alongside my studies, I work as a Decision Scientist on PayPal’s Partner Risk team, where I build statistical fraud-detection and anomaly-detection models. Previously, I was a research assistant in the AI, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Lab at TAU’s Coller School of Management, working on how human–AI collaboration shapes innovation.
